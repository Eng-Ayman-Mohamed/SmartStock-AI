================================================================================
  SmartStock AI — Assistant Test Questions
================================================================================

Use these questions to test the two search modes in the AI Chat page.
Set the mode dropdown to the appropriate mode before asking.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  MODE: Search Stock (NL Query)  —  Queries the inventory database
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1.  How many units do we have in stock for all products?

2.  Which items are currently below their reorder point and need restocking?

3.  Show me the top 5 products with the highest quantity on hand.

4.  What is the total inventory value across all active products?

5.  List all suppliers and their default lead time in days.

6.  Which SKUs have zero units on hand (completely out of stock)?

7.  Show me all products in the "Electronics" category (if seeded) or
    show me the first 10 products alphabetically.

8.  What are the sales numbers for the most recently sold product?

9.  Forecast demand for the product with the lowest stock level.

10. Show me all purchase orders that are currently in "approved" status
    and their total cost.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  MODE: Search Documents (RAG)  —  Searches the uploaded PDF documents
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1.  What was SmartStock's total revenue in Q2 2026?

2.  Which warehouse has the highest utilization rate and what is it?

3.  What is the return rate for clothing items and what is the
    most common reason for returns?

4.  Who is the contact person for the AI & Data Science department
    and what is their email?

5.  Which supplier has been flagged for declining reliability and
    why?

6.  What is the overall MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error) for
    the AI forecasting models in Q2 2026?

7.  What are the automated reorder rules for the top 20% fastest
    moving products?

8.  What is the shrinkage target and what triggers an auto-audit?

9.  What is SmartStock's CapEx plan for Q3 2026 and how much is
    budgeted in total?

10. What are the safety stock levels (in days) for groceries and
    electronics categories?
